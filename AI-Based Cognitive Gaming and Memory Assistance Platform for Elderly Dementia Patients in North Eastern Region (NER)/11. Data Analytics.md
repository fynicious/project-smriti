
# 27. What Analytics Should Be Tracked?

Analytics are the foundation of an AI-powered dementia care platform. They help determine whether a patient is improving, remaining stable, or experiencing cognitive decline. Rather than relying on occasional clinic visits, continuous analytics provide objective, longitudinal insights into cognition, health behaviors, and app engagement. Modern digital therapeutics increasingly use longitudinal behavioral, cognitive, and adherence metrics because they detect subtle changes earlier than infrequent assessments. [JMIR](https://www.jmir.org/2025/1/e73689?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

# 1. Cognitive Performance Analytics

These measure how the patient's brain performs during cognitive training.

### Memory Accuracy

- Percentage of correct answers in memory games
- Short-term memory recall
- Delayed recall after several minutes
- Recognition accuracy

Example:

- Week 1 → 68%
- Week 8 → 82%

Improvement indicates better memory retention.

---

### Attention Span

Measure:

- Time patient remains focused
- Number of distractions
- Missed targets
- Sustained attention score

Example:

Patient initially maintains attention for:

- 2 minutes

After training:

- 6 minutes

Longer sustained attention suggests improved cognitive function.

---

### Reaction Time

Measures processing speed.

Examples:

- Time taken to answer
- Touch response latency
- Decision-making speed

Example

Week 1

1.9 seconds

Week 12

1.1 seconds

A slower reaction time over months may indicate cognitive decline, while improvement can reflect successful training.

---

### Executive Function

Games can assess:

- Planning
- Sequencing
- Problem solving
- Logical reasoning

Metrics include:

- Completion rate
- Number of hints used
- Planning errors
- Time to solve puzzles

---

### Language Performance

Track:

- Object naming accuracy
- Word recall
- Sentence completion
- Verbal fluency

Language decline is an important marker in many dementia syndromes.

---

# 2. Long-Term Cognitive Trends

One day's score is not clinically meaningful.

The system should monitor performance over weeks and months.

Examples:

- Weekly cognitive score
- Monthly improvement percentage
- Rate of decline
- Performance stability
- Best and worst domains

The AI can generate a **Cognitive Health Score** by combining multiple cognitive measures into a single trend indicator, making it easier for caregivers and clinicians to interpret overall progress. Continuous longitudinal monitoring is a major advantage of digital cognitive assessment. [JMIR](https://www.jmir.org/2025/1/e73689?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

# 3. Health & Lifestyle Analytics

Brain health is influenced by daily habits.

### Medication Adherence

Track:

- Medicine taken
- Missed doses
- Late doses
- Weekly adherence percentage

Example

|Week|Adherence|
|---|---|
|1|96%|
|2|100%|
|3|72%|

Poor adherence often correlates with worsening health outcomes and is a key digital health metric. [PubMed Central (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12836306/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

### Hydration Compliance

Record:

- Daily water intake
- Missed hydration reminders
- Consecutive days meeting hydration goals

Hydration is important because dehydration can worsen confusion and cognitive symptoms in older adults.

---

### Sleep Analytics

Track:

- Sleep duration
- Bedtime consistency
- Wake-up time
- Sleep interruptions
- Sleep efficiency

Sleep disturbances are strongly associated with dementia progression and cognitive performance, making sleep one of the most valuable longitudinal metrics. [PubMed Central (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10332162/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

### Physical Activity (Optional)

If wearable devices are integrated:

Track:

- Daily steps
- Walking duration
- Activity level
- Sedentary time

Reduced physical activity may indicate declining health or increased frailty.

---

# 4. Emotional & Behavioral Analytics

Mood influences cognitive performance.

Daily mood check-ins may include:

😊 Happy

😐 Neutral

😔 Sad

😟 Anxious

The platform can monitor:

- Mood trends
- Mood variability
- Negative mood frequency
- Correlation between mood and game performance

Behavioral changes are clinically relevant because neuropsychiatric symptoms often accompany dementia progression. [JMIR mHealth and uHealth](https://mhealth.jmir.org/2026/1/e79469?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

# 5. Engagement Analytics

These evaluate whether the patient is actually using the platform.

Track:

- Daily logins
- Weekly active days
- Session duration
- Number of completed games
- Exercise completion rate
- Reminder response rate
- Feature usage
- Consecutive usage streak

Digital therapeutic research shows that engagement and adherence are closely linked to clinical benefit, making these metrics essential rather than optional. [PubMed Central (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12836306/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

# 6. AI-Powered Predictive Analytics

Instead of only displaying historical data, AI can generate predictions.

Examples:

### Cognitive Decline Prediction

Estimate:

- Low risk
- Moderate risk
- High risk

using historical performance trends.

---

### Personalized Difficulty Recommendation

AI automatically adjusts game difficulty based on:

- Accuracy
- Reaction time
- Mistakes
- Learning rate

This prevents boredom and frustration while maintaining optimal cognitive challenge.

---

### Early Warning Detection

Detect sudden changes such as:

- Rapid memory decline
- Reduced app usage
- Increased missed medications
- Poor sleep
- Persistent negative mood

The system can notify caregivers or healthcare professionals before these changes become severe.

---

# 7. Caregiver Analytics

Provide caregivers with actionable summaries rather than raw data.

Dashboard may display:

- Medication adherence (%)
- Sleep quality
- Mood trend
- Daily activity
- Missed reminders
- Cognitive score trend
- AI-generated risk alerts
- Weekly progress summary

---

# 8. Healthcare Professional Analytics

Clinicians require higher-level, evidence-based summaries.

Include:

- Cognitive trajectory over months
- Domain-wise performance (memory, attention, language, executive function)
- Medication compliance
- Sleep trends
- Mood trends
- Functional decline indicators
- AI risk predictions
- Recommended interventions
- Exportable progress reports for follow-up visits

---

# 9. System Analytics (App Performance)

For evaluating the platform itself:

- Daily Active Users (DAU)
- Weekly Active Users (WAU)
- Monthly Active Users (MAU)
- User retention (7-day, 30-day, 90-day)
- Average session length
- Feature adoption
- Crash rate
- Reminder effectiveness
- Notification open rate

These metrics help improve usability and long-term engagement. [PubMed Central (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12836306/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

# Recommended Analytics Framework

|Category|Metrics|
|---|---|
|**Cognitive**|Memory accuracy, attention span, reaction time, executive function, language performance|
|**Health**|Medication adherence, hydration, sleep duration, sleep efficiency, physical activity|
|**Behavioral**|Mood trend, emotional state, engagement level|
|**Usage**|Daily login, session duration, game completion, reminder response, feature usage|
|**AI Analytics**|Cognitive trend, decline prediction, adaptive difficulty, risk score, anomaly detection|
|**Caregiver Dashboard**|Compliance, alerts, mood, sleep, progress summaries|
|**Healthcare Dashboard**|Long-term cognitive trajectory, clinical reports, risk indicators, intervention recommendations|
|**System Analytics**|DAU, retention, session time, feature adoption, crash rate|

This combination provides a comprehensive view of **cognitive health, daily functioning, engagement, and disease progression**, aligning with current digital therapeutics and dementia monitoring research while supplying actionable insights for patients, caregivers, and healthcare professionals.
