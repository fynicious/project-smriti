
# 12. Why Multilingual Support?

## Answer

The North Eastern Region (NER) of India is one of the most linguistically diverse regions in the country. It is home to hundreds of languages and dialects, with many elderly individuals being far more comfortable communicating in their native language than in Hindi or English.

Commonly spoken languages include:

- Assamese
- Bodo
- Khasi
- Garo
- Manipuri (Meitei)
- Mizo
- Nagamese
- Kokborok
- Nepali
- Hindi
- English

For dementia patients, language plays a critical role in communication and cognitive functioning. As dementia progresses, many patients gradually lose proficiency in languages learned later in life while retaining stronger memories of their first language (mother tongue). As a result, instructions given in an unfamiliar language may become confusing, stressful, or difficult to follow.

Providing multilingual support allows patients to:

- Understand instructions more easily.
- Feel comfortable using the application.
- Reduce confusion and anxiety.
- Respond more accurately during cognitive exercises.
- Improve engagement and willingness to continue therapy.

For caregivers, multilingual interfaces also reduce the need for constant translation and make it easier to assist patients during training sessions.

### Benefits of Multilingual AI

AI-powered language support can:

- Deliver voice instructions in the patient's preferred language.
- Translate reminders automatically.
- Support speech recognition across multiple languages.
- Adapt game instructions based on language preference.
- Improve accessibility for users with limited literacy.

### Example

Instead of displaying:

> "Remember these five objects."

The application could present the same instruction in Assamese, Khasi, Mizo, or another preferred language, making the exercise easier to understand and more natural for the patient.

### Why It Matters

Language familiarity reduces cognitive load. When patients spend less effort understanding instructions, they can focus more on the actual memory or thinking task. This improves both performance and the effectiveness of cognitive training.

# 13. Why Use Cultural Themes?

## Answer

People remember information more effectively when it is familiar and personally meaningful. Dementia patients often retain long-term memories of places, traditions, foods, music, and cultural experiences from their earlier years, even when short-term memory has declined.

Many commercial cognitive games use objects and scenarios from Western cultures, such as:

- Apples
- Baseballs
- Snowmen
- Skyscrapers
- Thanksgiving meals

These may have little meaning for elderly individuals living in the North Eastern Region of India.

Instead, cognitive exercises can use locally familiar content such as:

- Bamboo forests
- Tea gardens
- Hornbill birds
- Traditional houses
- Local handicrafts
- Regional musical instruments
- Indigenous clothing
- Traditional festivals
- Local foods
- Village landscapes

Because these objects are already stored in long-term memory, patients are more likely to recognize and remember them.

### Benefits of Cultural Personalization

Using familiar cultural content:

- Increases recognition accuracy.
- Improves memory recall.
- Creates emotional comfort and familiarity.
- Makes games more enjoyable and engaging.
- Reduces frustration caused by unfamiliar objects.
- Encourages continued participation in therapy.

### Example

Instead of asking a patient to remember:

- Apple
- Pumpkin
- Snowman
- Baseball

The game could ask them to remember:

- Bamboo
- Tea leaves
- Hornbill
- Traditional drum

These items are more closely connected to the patient's daily life and long-term memories, making the activity more meaningful.

### Role of AI

Artificial Intelligence can personalize cultural content by learning the patient's:

- State or region
- Native language
- Cultural background
- Previous performance
- Familiar objects and themes

The system can then generate exercises that are both culturally relevant and cognitively appropriate for that individual.

### Why It Matters

Research in cognitive psychology shows that meaningful and emotionally significant information is generally recalled more effectively than unfamiliar information. By incorporating local culture into cognitive training, the platform becomes more engaging, accessible, and effective for elderly users in the North Eastern Region.

# 14. Why Daily Routine Recall?

## Answer

One of the major challenges faced by individuals with dementia is remembering everyday activities. Patients may forget whether they have taken their medication, eaten breakfast, locked the door, or attended a scheduled appointment. These difficulties reduce independence and increase the need for caregiver support.

Daily routine recall exercises are designed to strengthen **prospective memory**—the ability to remember to perform intended actions in the future.

Prospective memory is essential for independent living because it helps people remember tasks at the appropriate time rather than simply recalling past events.

### Examples of Daily Routine Tasks

Morning:

- Brush teeth
- Wash face
- Take medicine
- Drink water
- Eat breakfast
- Go for a walk

Afternoon:

- Eat lunch
- Drink water
- Rest
- Read a newspaper
- Attend therapy

Evening:

- Exercise
- Have dinner
- Take evening medicine
- Lock the doors
- Prepare for sleep

### How the Exercise Works

The application may display a sequence of daily activities and later ask the patient to:

- Recall the correct order.
- Identify missing steps.
- Choose the next appropriate activity.
- Match activities to the correct time of day.
- Respond to simulated reminders.

Example:

**Morning Routine**

1. Brush teeth
2. Take medicine
3. Eat breakfast
4. Drink water

Later, the game asks:

> "What comes after brushing your teeth?"

or

> "Which activity is missing from today's routine?"

These exercises repeatedly reinforce healthy daily habits.

### Role of AI

Artificial Intelligence can personalize routine recall by:

- Learning the patient's daily schedule.
- Tracking which routines are frequently forgotten.
- Increasing practice for weaker areas.
- Sending reminders at appropriate times.
- Adjusting exercise difficulty based on performance.
- Detecting declining routine recall over time.

### Benefits

Routine recall exercises help patients:

- Improve prospective memory.
- Strengthen attention and sequencing skills.
- Reinforce healthy daily habits.
- Reduce missed medications.
- Increase confidence in completing everyday tasks.
- Maintain independence for a longer period.

Caregivers also benefit because the system can identify recurring difficulties and provide targeted reminders or alerts when additional support may be needed.

### Why It Matters

Although cognitive games cannot cure dementia, regularly practicing familiar daily routines helps preserve essential life skills.
