# 32. Success Metrics (KPIs)

A healthcare AI solution must measure **clinical impact, engagement, caregiver outcomes, and system reliability**. These metrics demonstrate whether the platform is genuinely improving patients' quality of life.

|KPI|Why it Matters|Target|
|---|---|---|
|Memory game accuracy|Indicates memory performance|↑ 15–25% over baseline|
|Attention span|Measures sustained focus|Increasing trend|
|Reaction time|Detects slowing cognition|Stable or improved|
|Cognitive assessment trend|Tracks long-term progression|Slower decline than baseline|
|Daily cognitive exercises completed|Measures adherence to therapy|>80% completion|

|KPI|Goal|
|---|---|
|Medication adherence|>90%|
|Hydration compliance|>85%|
|Routine completion|>80%|

# Core Problem Statement (Refined)

> **Design and develop an offline-first, AI-powered, multilingual digital cognitive care platform for elderly individuals with dementia in the North Eastern Region of India. The platform combines adaptive cognitive training, culturally relevant content, voice-first interaction, medication and wellness reminders, on-device AI personalization, and secure caregiver and healthcare dashboards to improve cognitive engagement, treatment adherence, early detection of decline, and overall quality of life while ensuring accessibility, privacy, and reliable operation in low-connectivity environments.**

## What can make this an SIH-winning solution?

To stand out beyond the baseline requirements, consider these differentiators:

- **Digital Twin of Cognitive Health:** Maintain a continuously updated cognitive profile that adapts recommendations based on longitudinal performance rather than isolated sessions.
- **Explainable AI:** Show caregivers and clinicians _why_ the AI generated a risk score or recommendation (e.g., reduced memory accuracy, slower reaction time, increased missed medications).
- **Adaptive Accessibility:** Dynamically adjust font size, contrast, button size, speech speed, and interaction complexity based on observed user behavior.
- **Multimodal AI:** Combine game performance, voice interactions, reminder responses, optional activity data, and mood logs to improve personalization instead of relying on a single signal.
- **Offline Clinical Decision Support:** Allow healthcare workers to review summarized patient trends even in low-connectivity settings, synchronizing once connectivity returns.
- **Federated Learning (Future Roadmap):** Improve AI models using decentralized updates from devices without uploading raw patient data, enhancing privacy.
- **Cultural Personalization Engine:** Adapt games, stories, music, festivals, and languages to each user's community and preferences, increasing engagement and recall.
- **Emergency Intelligence:** Detect prolonged inactivity, repeated missed medications, or unusual behavior patterns and escalate alerts based on configurable thresholds rather than single events.

These additions demonstrate innovation, practical deployment considerations, and responsible AI—qualities that SIH evaluation panels typically value highly.
